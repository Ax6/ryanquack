Ryanair says I need the app. Well, quack that — I'll get the ticket my way. 🦆

A free, open-source browser extension that fetches, displays, and downloads your Ryanair boarding passes directly in your browser. No app needed.

Ryanair moved boarding passes into its app. RyanQuack paddles upstream — connecting to your own booking to render your pass right in your browser.

Features:
🎟️ Instant boarding pass — QR code rendered, scan-ready for the gate
🍏 Apple Wallet export — download your pass for your iPhone
🖼️ Image export — save a high-res PNG to print or share
📦 Bulk download — all passengers' passes at once, bundled in a zip
✈️ Upcoming flights — see your trips before check-in opens
🔒 Privacy-focused — runs locally; nothing is sent to any third-party server
📖 Open source — fully auditable, no hidden tricks
